Introduction

The Pennsylvania Native Plant Initiative is a program dedicated to promoting the use of native plants in landscaping and gardening. Our goal is to educate the public about the benefits of native plants and to encourage their use in residential and commercial landscapes.


Featured Plant

Sunflowers are a popular native plant in Pennsylvania. They are known for their bright yellow flowers and their ability to attract pollinators such as bees and butterflies. Sunflowers are also easy to grow and can thrive in a variety of soil types.
